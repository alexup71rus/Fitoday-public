Fitoday Support

Help with your home workouts in Fitoday.

Contact the developer

If something is not working

Tell us what happened and which step you were on. Include your device model, operating system version and Fitoday version. Attach a screenshot if it helps explain the issue. Do not send personal photos or workout history unless necessary.

No account or internet needed

You do not need an account to work out. Your plan, workout history and settings are stored on your device. Sending an email to support requires an internet connection.

Moving or deleting your data

In Settings, you can save your data to a file or restore it from a file. You can also delete all app data there. Previously exported files and system backups must be deleted separately.

Languages and voice guidance

Text is available in Russian, English and Simplified Chinese. Voice guidance is available in Russian and English. Chinese uses English voice guidance. Choose a language in Settings or follow your system language.
